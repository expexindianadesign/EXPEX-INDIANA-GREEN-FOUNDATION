Who we are

Our vision is a world where growth gives back to nature.

EXPEX INDIANA GREEN FOUNDATION is an environmental initiative built around practical community action. Our focus is not only planting trees, but helping them survive and supporting the people and ecosystems around them.

We aim to work with local communities, schools, volunteers, environmental groups and responsible partners. Every activity should be documented, measurable and rooted in local needs.

Our action areas

From planting to long-term care.

Ideas for meaningful programmes that can be developed with local partners and communities.

Afforestation Drives

Plant locally suitable native trees on approved sites, with community participation and a survival-monitoring plan.

Water for Drought-Prone Areas

Support rainwater harvesting, recharge pits, watershed restoration and water budgeting with local experts.

Community Green Teams

Work with schools, farmers and residents to care for saplings, protect existing trees and keep local green spaces healthy.

Adopt & Protect a Tree

Assign a care group to water, mulch and monitor each planting site, especially through the first dry seasons.

Green Schools

Run nature-learning sessions, campus biodiversity activities, seed collection and student-led tree-care clubs.

Urban Micro-Forests

Explore native dense-planting projects, including Miyawaki-style sites, only where land, water and maintenance are suitable.
